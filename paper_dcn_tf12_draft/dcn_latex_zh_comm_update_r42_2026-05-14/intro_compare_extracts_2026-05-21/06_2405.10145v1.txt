I. INTRODUCTION 
ONLINEAR behaviors are difficult to characterize with 
physical-based models, and even if it is possible, the 
expressions are quite complicated [1]–[3]. Besides, many well- 
established control techniques are based on linear systems 
or input affine systems, whereas mechatronic systems, par- 
ticularly those classes with high nonlinear dynamics, such 
as vehicles, cannot satisfy such prerequisites or assumptions. 
Thereby, this limits the application and extension of methods. 
Data-driven modelling methodology provides a tutorial on 
these issues. The data-driven method does not rely on the 
internal structure and parameters of the control plant but rather 
on the input and output data, which can effectively characterize 
the nonlinear components and improve the model fidelity of 
the system [4]–[7].
