# Sources Used

- LetPub journal search/detail pages; URLs are in `journal_candidates.csv`.
- OpenAlex Works API: https://api.openalex.org/works
- Unpaywall API when an email is configured: https://unpaywall.org/products/api
- DOI and publisher landing pages are recorded in article tables.

Closed articles are not downloaded through paywall bypass. Use visible metadata, abstracts, DOI pages, and legal OA links only.
